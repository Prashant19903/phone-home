# Changelog

## 0.4.0
- Add / remove / drag-to-reorder tiles, 10 presets and custom `https://` sites (optional per-site permission).
- Auto-fit phones to the window.
- Colourful aurora background that tints to the hovered app.
- Tile buttons show on hover only.
- Builder Bot mascot (can be switched off).
- Two editions from one source: Full (live frames) and Lite (phone-sized windows, storage permission only).
- README, MIT license, build script.

## 0.3.0
- Apple TV style UI: glass phones, focus lift and glow, clock and greeting.

## 0.2.0
- Facebook / WhatsApp: make the frame request look like a top-level navigation.
- "Pop out" button (phone-sized window) as a fallback for sites that refuse frames.

## 0.1.0
- First prototype: Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, WhatsApp in phone-shaped frames on the new tab page.
